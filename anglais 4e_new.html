📚 Vocabulaire

Ex 1: Vêtements

Traduis: robe, chemise, pantalon, chaussures, manteau, chapeau, ceinture, gants

Ex 2: La maison

Complète: bedroom, kitchen, ___(salon), bathroom, ___(escalier), garage, ___(grenier)

Ex 3: Hobbies

Traduis: lire, danser, peindre, jouer de la musique, faire du sport, jouer aux jeux vidéo, écouter

Ex 4: Nourriture

Complète: apple, bread, cheese, ___(poisson), meat, ___(légumes), water, ___(jus), milk

Ex 5: Émotions

Traduis: heureux, triste, en colère, surpris, fatigué, enthousiaste, nerveux, content

Ex 6: Transport

Traduis: voiture, bus, train, avion, vélo, moto, taxi, bateau, métro

✏️ Grammaire - Présent Avancé

Ex 7: Present Progressive

Transforme: I study English → ___. She watches TV → ___. They eat breakfast → ___.

Ex 8: Present Perfect avec for/since

Complète: I have lived here ___(depuis) 10 years. She has studied ___(pendant) 5 hours.

Ex 9: Comparatifs

Mets au comparatif et superlatif: big → ___, ___. beautiful → ___, ___. good → ___, ___

Ex 10: Adverbes de fréquence

Place correctement: I (always) go to school. She (never) eats meat. They (often) play football.

Ex 11: Pronoms relatifs

Complète avec who/which/that: The girl ___ is my friend. The car ___ is red. The book ___ I read.

Ex 12: Cas génératif

Transforme: The book of John → ___. The car of my father → ___. The house of my friend → ___

Ex 13: Some/Any/Much/Many

Complète: There are ___ apples. She doesn't have ___ pen. How ___ money do you have?

Ex 14: Articles A/An

Complète avec a/an: ___ apple, ___ orange, ___ umbrella, ___ hour, ___ dog, ___ cat, ___ elephant

⏰ Grammaire - Passé Avancé

Ex 15: Passé régulier/irrégulier

Complète: I studied, she ___(go), they ___(eat), we ___(play), he ___(see), you ___(make)

Ex 16: Past Progressive

Transforme: I studied → I ___ studying. She worked → She ___ working. He slept → ___

Ex 17: Past Perfect

Complète: Before she left, she ___(finish) homework. When I arrived, they ___(already/leave).

Ex 18: Discours rapporté

Transforme: "I am happy" → He said that ___. "She will come" → She said that ___.

Ex 19: Questions indirectes

Transforme: "Where do you live?" → He asked where ___. "What is your name?" → She wanted to know ___.

Ex 20: Used to

Transforme: I lived in Paris → I ___ in Paris. She played tennis → She ___ tennis.

⚙️ Verbes Modaux

Ex 21: Can/Could/Must/Should

Complète: I ___(pouvoir) swim. You ___(devoir) study. She ___(should) help. They ___(pouvaient) walk.

Ex 22: Permission/Obligation

Utilise le modal: Tu peux aller? → ___. Tu dois étudier. → ___. C'est possible? → ___.

Ex 23: Might/May/Will/Shall

Complète: It ___(might) rain. You ___(may) come. I ___(will) help. We ___(shall) go?

Ex 24: Have to/Need to

Transforme: I must go → ___. She should study → ___. We have to leave → ___.

📖 Compréhension Écrite

Ex 25: Texte avec contexte

Lis et réponds:Last summer, I went to the beach with my family. We spent three weeks in a coastal town. Every day, we swam and played. In evenings, we walked and watched the sunset. It was the best holiday! Questions: Where did they go? How long? What did they do?

Ex 26: Multiple choice

Sélectionne la bonne réponse selon le texte présenté. (Texte court fourni, choix multiples)

Ex 27: Remplissage de trous

Remplis: My school is very ___. We have many ___. My favorite subject is ___. I have ___ with friends.

Ex 28: Annonce

Lis l'annonce:SUMMER CAMP - Swimming, sports, arts, music. July 1st to August 15th. Cost: $500. Questions: What activities? When? Cost?
