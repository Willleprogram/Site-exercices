📚 Vocabulaire de Base

Ex 1: Salutations

Traduis: Bonjour, au revoir, s'il vous plaît, merci, excusez-moi, comment allez-vous?, oui, non

Ex 2: L'école

Complète: classroom, teacher, student, book, ___(cahier), ___(stylo), desk, ___(tableau), pen

Ex 3: Les sports

Traduis: football, basket, tennis, natation, course à pied, cyclisme, volleyball, cricket

Ex 4: Fruits/Légumes

Traduis: pomme, banane, orange, fraise, carotte, tomate, concombre, pomme de terre

Ex 5: Animaux de compagnie

Traduis: chien, chat, poisson, hamster, lapin, oiseau, tortue, souris

Ex 6: Les couleurs

Traduis: rouge, bleu, vert, jaune, noir, blanc, gris, orange, rose

✏️ Grammaire - Présent

Ex 7: "To be"

Complète: I ___ (suis) French. You ___ (êtes) English. He ___ (est) a student. We ___ English.

Ex 8: Verbes réguliers

Conjugue "to like": I like, she ___, we ___, they ___, he ___, you ___

Ex 9: Présent négatif

Mets à la forme négative: I like chocolate. She plays tennis. He works here. They like pizza.

Ex 10: Questions

Forme des questions: Do you like football? Does she speak English? Where do they live?

Ex 11: "There is/are"

Complète: ___ (Il y a) a cat. ___ (Il y a) ten students. ___ (Il y a) books on the table.

Ex 12: Adjectifs possessifs

Remplis: This is my book. That is ___(sa) pen. These are ___(leurs) bags. Your name is ___

Ex 13: Présent progressif

Transforme: I read → I ___ reading. She plays → ___. They work → ___. He studies → ___

Ex 14: Can (pouvoir)

Complète: I ___ swim. She ___ play piano. They ___ speak French. We ___ run fast.

⏰ Grammaire - Passé

Ex 15: "To be" passé

Conjugue au passé: I ___, you ___, he ___, we ___, they ___, she ___

Ex 16: Verbes réguliers passé

Mets au passé simple: I play → ___. She walks → ___. They talk → ___. He works → ___

Ex 17: Verbes irréguliers

Complète: I went, she took, he did, they ___, we ___, she ___, I was, they were

Ex 18: Passé négatif

Mets à la forme négative: I went to school. She saw a movie. He ate lunch. They played football.

Ex 19: Questions passé

Forme des questions: Did you go to the park? What did she eat? Where did they go?

Ex 20: Mots de temps

Remplis: I went to the beach ___. She started school ___(il y a) two years. They moved ___ week.

🔮 Grammaire - Futur

Ex 21: Futur avec will

Transforme au futur: I study → ___. She plays → ___. They work → ___. He eats → ___

Ex 22: Going to

Transforme: I eat an apple → I ___ eating. She watches TV → ___. They play → ___

Ex 23: Futur négatif

Mets à la forme négative: I will come. She will study. They will play. He will help.

Ex 24: Questions futur

Forme des questions: Will you come? What will you do? When will they arrive? Where will she go?

📖 Compréhension Écrite

Ex 25: Présentation

Lis:Hello, my name is Sarah. I am 11 years old. I live in London. I go to school every day. I like football and reading. My favorite color is blue.
Réponds: What is her name? How old? What does she like?

Ex 26: Dialogue

Complète: A: "Hello! What is your name?" B: "My name is ___." A: "Nice to meet you." B: "Nice to meet you ___!"

Ex 27: Vrai/Faux

Texte: My family went to the beach. We played and swam. We had lunch.
V/F: They went to the beach. They played football. They swam. They ate.

Ex 28: Associe

Relie: What is your name? → a) I am 12 / How old? → b) My name is Tom / Where from? → c) England
